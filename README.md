# SmartBudget Manager

**Purpose:** SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook for recording monthly income and expenses, grouping expenses by category, and checking whether spending stays within a budget.

## What is included?
- **Dashboard:** total income, total expenses, monthly balance, budget status, category summary, and chart.
- **Transactions:** enter each income or expense, including date, description, type, category, amount, and notes.
- **Categories:** predefined categories and monthly budget amounts that users can review or edit.

## Requirements
- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, Microsoft 365, or a compatible spreadsheet application.
- The provided `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx` workbook.

## Get started
1. Make a copy of `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx` and open the copy in Excel.
2. Select **Transactions**.
3. Enter each transaction on a new row. Choose **Income** or **Expense** in Type and select a category for expenses.
4. Enter the amount in the matching Income or Expense column; do not overwrite formula cells in the Month column.
5. Open **Categories** to review or change the monthly budgets.
6. Open **Dashboard** to review the totals, balance, category spending, chart, and budget status.
7. Save your workbook regularly.

## Important notes
- Enter an amount in the Income column for income records and in the Expense column for expense records.
- Keep category names consistent so category totals are calculated correctly.
- Avoid deleting formulas, drop-down lists, or formatting.
- The workbook summarizes information entered by the user; it does not connect to bank accounts or verify transactions.
